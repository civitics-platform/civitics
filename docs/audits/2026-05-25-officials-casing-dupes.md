## Notes

Cluster definition is `lower(trim(full_name))` only. Some clusters are
genuine namesakes (different people sharing a common name), especially
the candidate-heavy ones from FEC cn{yy}.zip ingest. The merge spec
(FIX-A) must therefore pick clusters whose internal rows share at
least one of: a stable external ID across rows, an overlapping
`external_relationships` neighborhood (shared edges in LittleSis),
or matching state + role family — not raw name match alone.

Per the FIX-325 prompt, clusters whose tier-array contains both
`elected` and `candidate` for **different real people** are
out-of-scope for FIX-A — those collide via the FIX-248 promotion
path, and merging them blind would conflate distinct identities.
