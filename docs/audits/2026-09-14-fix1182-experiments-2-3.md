### The decomposition against FIX-1106's figure

The bullet's 3,416 / 209,017 / $1,481,928,516 was measured on 2026-08-25 by the
bracket-row proxy over **two** sources — `metadata->>'source' in
(fec_bulk_indiv, fec_bulk_indiv_to_committee)` — and split 2,736 financial
entities + 680 officials. Each source carries exactly one `to_type`, so the
proxy's two halves map to the two sources one-for-one: officials are the indiv
arm, financial entities are the indiv-to-committee arm. `fec_bulk_pac` was never
in the proxy's scope at all.

| | proxy, 2026-08-25 | exact (DROPPED-OUT), 2026-09-14 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| officials ← `fec_bulk_indiv` | 680 | **128** | 5.3× over |
| financial entities ← `fec_bulk_indiv_to_committee` | 2,736 | **626** | 4.4× over |
| **recipients, two-source total** | **3,416** | **754** | 4.5× over |
| **rows** | **209,017** | **174,211** | 1.20× over |
| **dollars** | **$1,481,928,516** | **$643,491,158** | 2.30× over |

Adding PARTIAL back (the class the emit set does *not* fully support, kept
separate deliberately) brings the two-source row count to **199,857** — within
**4.4%** of the proxy's 209,017. So the proxy was close on ROWS and badly wrong
on RECIPIENTS and DOLLARS: it spread roughly the right volume of rows across
4.5× too many recipients, and the dollar figure inherited that error.

`fec_bulk_pac`, which the bullet never covered, adds a further **134 officials /
20,165 rows / $70,305,398** of DROPPED-OUT residue.

**These numbers replace the bullet's.** The FIX-1106 figure should be read as
754 recipients / 174,211 rows / $643.5M across the two sources it described,
plus 134 / 20,165 / $70.3M on a third it did not.

## 2. Experiment (3) — Susan Collins, and what the DROPPED-OUT class is made of

The prompt framed this as "if her committee received emitted indiv-to-committee
rows, the class is at least partly a match gap." The officials-side answer came
back stronger and different, and it **refutes the premise of FIX-1182's own
example**.

### There are two Susan Collins rows, and both hold `S6ME00159`

| id | full_name | role_title | updated_at |
|---|---|---|---|
| `3e559d5c-0763-4f3f-b654-7ae93f1a2a93` | Susan M. Collins | **Senator** | 2026-09-13 22:45:19 |
| `c6825cc2-d15c-4e81-a3d3-a647ab0466e7` | Susan Collins | **Candidate for Senator** | 2026-08-10 22:25:21 |

Both `role_title`s are in `FEC_ELECTABLE_ROLE_TITLES`, and both map to office
`S`, which is `S6ME00159`'s prefix. **The FIX-937/1025 gate excludes neither.**

### Cycle-2026 `fec_bulk_indiv` rows, and whether run `84470d3b` emitted them

| id | rows | cents | last_write | emitted by `84470d3b` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `3e559d5c` (Senator) | 5,243 | $8,879,021 | 2026-08-24 | **5,243 — all of them** |
| `c6825cc2` (Candidate) | 3,248 | $6,033,338 | 2026-07-27 | **0** |

So the **$6.03M the FIX-1182 bullet calls "a sitting senator's" is on the
candidate DUPLICATE, not on the sitting senator.** The sitting senator's own
$8.88M is fully emitted and is not in any manifest. The money did not stop
existing; the matcher stopped resolving `S6ME00159` to the candidate row and now
resolves it to the elected row. That is a duplicate-official artefact, and the
3,248 stale rows are exactly the double-count FIX-1106 exists to remove.

Her committees tell the same story from the other side — all three are
DROPPED-OUT on the indiv-to-committee arm with zero emitted keys:

| committee | FEC id | cycle-2026 ITC rows | cents | emitted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COLLINS VICTORY COMMITTEE | C00692897 | 694 | $2,912,054 | 0 |
| COLLINS VICTORY FUND | C00824151 | 420 | $1,456,227 | 0 |
| SUSAN COLLINS FOR MAINE | C00920389 | 16 | $338,500 | 0 |

(Her historical principal campaign committee, COLLINS FOR SENATOR / C00314575,
has no `financial_entities` row on prod at all.)

### The cohort — this is the part that matters

Collins is not a special case. Taking all **128** DROPPED-OUT officials from the
indiv manifest and asking whether another `officials` row holds the *same*
`fec_candidate_id` and *was* emitted to on 09-13:

| | officials | rows | $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| has a same-CAND_ID sibling that WAS emitted | **88** | 46,561 | **$72,951,060** |
| no emitted sibling | **40** | 21,372 | $33,694,265 |
| *(of the 128, 95 have a same-CAND_ID sibling at all; 1 has no CAND_ID)* | | | |
| **total** | **128** | **67,933** | **$106,645,325** |

**68.4% of the DROPPED-OUT dollars are provably a migration between two
`officials` rows for the same candidate, not a retraction.**

Breaking the remaining 40 down by the FIX-1025 role↔office rule:

| verdict | officials | rows | $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| role and office agree — no structural explanation | 34 | 18,533 | $30,261,581 |
| **role/office MISMATCH** — the rule refuses the stored id | 5 | 2,650 | $3,168,202 |
| no `fec_candidate_id` at all | 1 | 189 | $264,482 |

The 5 mismatches are a named mechanism, and a live one: **Roger Marshall
(`role_title` Senator, holding `H6KS01179`) and Adam B. Schiff (Senator, holding
`H0CA27085`)** are House members who moved to the Senate. Their `officials` row
was updated to `Senator`; their stored `fec_candidate_id` is still the House id;
`roleMayHoldFecOffice("Senator", "H")` is false. They stopped being matchable
against their own historical money.

### Verdict for FIX-1182

**Match gap, not retraction, for at least 71% of the class** — $76.1M of the
$106.6M is explained today as a binding artefact (the 88 duplicates plus the 5
role/office mismatches). The genuinely undecided residue is **34 officials /
18,533 rows / $30.26M**, and *that* is what Sunday 2026-09-20's second complete
emit run has to decide. It is a materially smaller and better-characterised
question than the one the bullet poses.

The apply stays blocked. The 88-duplicate class is almost certainly correct to
delete, but "almost certainly" is not the standard this manifest is held to, and
the same run that settles the 34 will settle them.

Filed from this: **FIX-1187** — both mechanisms, the duplicate-official migration
and the role/office mismatch.
