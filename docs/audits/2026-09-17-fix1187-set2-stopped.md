### 2a and 2c are not waitable either, and that is worth saying

`partial` is the **designed terminal status** of the EC bounded-crawl arm: each
firing runs one unit, hits its cap, and records "unit cap reached — 1 unit(s) run;
resumable at arm entity_co...". Over 30 hours prod logged **one** complete EC unit
(2026-09-16 19:00) against dozens of partial and skipped. A precondition of "none
partial" describes a state prod does not visit.

2c is stricter than the schedule permits. EC's only vacuum owner is
`ec-vacuum-analyze` at **04:30 UTC daily**. It was last vacuumed 2026-09-17
05:19:41 at 0 dead / 100 %; 18 h 04 m later it is at 457,118 dead — roughly
25,000 dead tuples an hour. Reaching <= 5,000 means starting within about twelve
minutes of the vacuum, but the same read forbids starting within **90 minutes** of
04:25-05:00, and the 05:45-09:00 blackout pushes the earliest legal start to 09:00
UTC, by which time EC carries four and a half hours of crawl churn. **The EC
dead-tuple gate and the vacuum-proximity gate cannot both be satisfied.** Whatever
replaces this precondition has to be written against a vacuum that actually runs
near the window, or stated as a ratio rather than an absolute.

## 3. What actually happened to Marshall

`promote_candidate_to_elected(p_elected_id, p_candidate_id)` promotes the
**candidate** row in place, rewrites about twenty tables' FKs onto it, and ends
with `DELETE FROM officials WHERE id = p_elected_id`. Its identity merge is:

```sql
SELECT (c.source_ids || e.source_ids) INTO v_merged_source_ids
```

with the comment, three lines above, saying *"we want (candidate || elected) —
candidate's fec_candidate_id is preserved, elected's congress_gov is appended."*
`jsonb || jsonb` keeps the **right** operand on key conflict. The code therefore
does the opposite of its comment: the **elected** row's `fec_candidate_id` wins and
the candidate's is destroyed. That is [[FIX-1195]].

Applied to Marshall on 2026-09-17 at about 23:20 UTC:

- candidate `60f87348` holding `fec_candidate_id: S0KS00315` promoted to `tier='elected'`
- merged with the elected row's `congress_gov: M001198, fec_candidate_id: H6KS01179`
  so **H6KS01179 wins and S0KS00315 is erased**
- `27090d3a`, set 1's survivor, deleted, its 189 FR rows moved across (4,678 to 4,867)

### It was not one row

Three of set 1's 151 survivors are gone:

| official | deleted survivor | promoted row | CAND_ID erased | promoted at (UTC) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mark Harris | `afc05fd0` | `c0af0396` | **H6NC09200** | 23:19:14 |
| Robert Menendez | `ac7de9f0` | `747c96d0` | **H2NJ13075** | 23:19:32 |
| Roger Marshall | `27090d3a` | `60f87348` | **S0KS00315** | about 23:20:28 |

All three erased ids read **0 live / 0 merged / 0 prior** holders on prod. The
local pre-promotion clone still carries each one — that is the only reason they
are recoverable.

### Why exactly these three, and why now

`promote-candidates` matches on `(normName(full_name), state, role_family)` and
**skips unless exactly one candidate matches** — the guard reads
`if (matches.length !== 1) continue;`.

These three are the only set-1 pairs whose elected and candidate `full_name`
strings are *identical* — "Roger Marshall" against "Roger Marshall", where the
other 148 differ by a middle initial ("Adam B. Schiff" against "Adam Schiff").
Before set 1 each of the three had **two** candidate rows on that key, so the match
count was 2 and the promotion skipped them every night. Set 1 retired one stub's
`fec_candidate_id`; the candidate load filters on `fec_candidate_id NOT NULL`; that
stub dropped out of the index; the match count became 1; and the promotion fired on
the first nightly afterwards. **Set 1 is what unblocked it.** That is [[FIX-1196]].

### The blast radius is bounded

Of the 148 surviving set-1 survivors, **147 now have 0 candidate matches and 1 has
2 — none sit at exactly 1**, so no further promotions are queued from that cohort.
The damage stopped at three.

## 4. What this costs set 2

The manifest cannot be repaired by editing Marshall's row, because the question it
answered has changed. It was written to move a Senate id onto a survivor that held
a House id. For Marshall there is now one row holding a House id and **no row
holding the Senate id at all** — `S0KS00315` has to be *restored* before it can be
*moved*, and restoring it is FIX-1195's work, not a merge. Harris and Menendez are
not in set 2 but are in the same state for their own erased ids.

Set 2 should be re-derived from a fresh prod read after FIX-1195 and FIX-1196 are
resolved, not re-attempted. The four unaffected rows (Schiff, Peters, Curtis,
Lummis) are still exactly as cc-129 left them and their FR counts match to the row,
so they will survive re-derivation unchanged.
