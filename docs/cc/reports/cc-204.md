  22:30Z. The DB was *unreachable from outside* (REST 522, pooler timeout,

  is false.** A clean shutdown persists pgss: `stats_reset` still reads 09-22
  22:32:13Z, so there is no fresh epoch, and R8's "fresh epoch" read has nothing

- **R7(a)'s `--by sql_state_code` cannot name the shape.** It is scoped to 57014

  LOG, 2 × 08006, 2 × 57P01 (the fast shutdown) and 1 × 57014. There was no
  53200 and no 57P02.

  is silent from 16:04:05Z to 00:46:35Z, including the three hours when pg_cron
  was demonstrably running and logging. Log shipping went dark with the ingress.
  The `database system is ready to accept connections` line the prompt expected
  is absent even after the 00:48:31Z start.

  outage, not the Postgres one. The probe series (`cron.job_run_details`) is the

  fork". The cancel was due ~19:00Z and landed 21:03:37Z. The cause is unread
