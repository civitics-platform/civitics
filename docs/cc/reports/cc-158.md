# cc-158: the whale donor page, measured

Written for Craig and the Cowork reviewer. The full tables and receipts are in `docs/audits/2026-09-25-fix1217-whale-donor-page.md` (+ `.json`). **Nothing landed on prod. STOPPED for your pick; cc-159 lands it.**

## Summary

1. **The bullet's four-column index doesn't work for anon.**
   - The planner takes `(to_type, to_id, relationship_type, amount_cents DESC)`, but as anon it **can't use the index's order**.
   - `relationship_type` is an enum, and `enum_eq` is not leakproof on clone or prod. Under RLS, a non-leakproof equality never becomes an equivalence-class constant, so the index can't satisfy `ORDER BY amount_cents`.
   - On the clone, RNC `inbound` went from **1,501 → 5,634 ms cold**: all 151,406 rows, then a sort. The contractor whale's `spending` read took **15.3 s**.
   - **Partial predicates work where key columns don't.** Both partials flip to `Limit ← Index Scan`.
2. **The page is wrong, not just slow, for ordinary recipients too.**
   - On the clone, the top-50 set is wrong for **94 of the 328 recipients over 1,000 rows**, including 19 of 167 in the 1k–2k band.
   - The RNC misses 6 of its true top 50 (16 of 50 at the right rank).
   - The "Donors" stat is the sample's distinct count for every recipient over 1k rows. The RNC shows 837 against 125,567.
   - Filed as **FIX-1225**.
3. **"Total received" is $0 on prod** for 34 of the 41 largest recipients, including the RNC, DNC and DSCC.
   - The unscoped PAC entity upsert writes a literal 0 on conflict.
   - The FR-dirty `fe-crawl` never re-derives a committee whose FR rows didn't change.
   - Filed as **FIX-1226**.
4. **Recommendation:**
   - **A partial spending index** plus **an FE inbound rollup** carrying `donor_count` / `tx_total` / `sum_total`.
   - Both the `donor-count` read and the 1,000-row sample go away.
   - The no-DDL exact aggregate was timed on prod: **cancelled at 3,026 ms** for the RNC, cold.

## 4. What contradicted the bullet or this prompt

1. **The planner takes the four-column index for `count: exact` as an index-only scan, with Heap Fetches 0** (clone VM 100 %, prod 99.91 %). But the index **can't bound** `inbound` or `spending` for anon, because `enum_eq` isn't leakproof. The bullet's "index range scans bounded by their LIMIT" is false under RLS.
2. **The whale class is about a hundred, not four committees:** 94 FEs over 10k inbound rows of any type on prod, and 828 over 1k. It includes **contractors with zero donations**, whose `donor-count` read is the worst of all (14.4 s cold on the clone). cc-151 saw only party committees because only they were visited.
3. **Mid-size recipients are wrong.** In the 1k–2k band, 19 of 167 have a wrong top-50 set (minimum overlap 36 of 50), and the "Donors" stat averages 0.73 of the truth.
4. **The from-side (`outbound`) doesn't share the whale shape** (clone max 2,279 rows per donor). It has the same code shape, so the Recipients stat is a sample for the 75 donors over 1k. Out of scope; noted in FIX-1225.
5. **Design B's `received_tx_count` on `financial_entities` would sit beside a column the PAC upsert clobbers** (FIX-1226). B carries its own summary instead, and needs no FE writer change. Design B's min/max cycle columns aren't needed either: inbound cycles render only when there are no inbound rows (`page.tsx:663-667`).
6. **The RNC has 337,058 rows on prod, not ~94,576.** That was a clone plan estimate, and as anon the planner uses default selectivity.
7. **`official_donor_rollup_mv` is 656,050 rows on prod, not 2,972,870**, and it holds no FE recipients.
8. **"Hours on Pro Small"** for the build is ≤ ~19 min at the measured rate.
9. **Template rule (g) quotes `fr-vacuum-analyze` at 161–212 s; it now runs ~10 s.** By the template's own proportional rule, that moves it to the ≥ 10-min spacing class.
10. **ISR (`revalidate = 300`) can cache a timed-out render** with an empty donor list and "0" stats for 5 minutes. Not measured; noted for cc-159's page change.
