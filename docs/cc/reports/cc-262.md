## The three hypotheses

- **H1 (the post-landing tail burns the budget): HELD in shape, undecidable in
  mechanism.** The tail cost ~5× the landing (17 % → 88 %), and the second
  ecs pass spent 9 % in 90 min on 11-03. Whether 11-02 emptied the instance
  bucket (the dashboard says 88, not 100) waits for a throttled day under the
  sampler.
- **H2 (platform-counts on a dirty VM): HELD as a contributor, not the cause.**
  EC (VM 0.848) and entity_tags (VM 0.535) count by **Seq Scan**: EC three
  times (count, filtered count, the `connection_type` GROUP BY), plus
  entity_tags' 1 GB. FR and FE use an Index Only Scan at VM 1.000. Mondays
  after a landing ran 40.3 / 48.6 / 102.0 s against 20–69 s otherwise. 11-02's
  2,065.9 s cancel is 20× the worst of those: the throttle, not the VM. The
  cancel rolled back whole (one transaction), so `platform_counts` reads
  11-01 03:53Z.
- **H3 (the post-landing stack): HELD, and it is the cause.** The ec-crawl's
  `entity_connection_stats_windows` costs **~35 s a window on Sundays and
  ~250 s after a landing**. One window reads **~8.8 GB from `/data`** at the
  volume's IOPS ceiling, so a 16-window pass reads on the order of 140 GB.
  pgss puts the ec-crawl at 341.5 of the 1,047.9 GB read since 10-25. The
  pass began 03:01Z. Platform-counts and the vacuums' reads landed on it at
  03:53Z, and then the dispatcher stacked four more units in. 08-31 and 09-01
  had the same shape, with **the same statements cancelled**

## What contradicted the prompt

1. **"Two stalls" was one throttle, and it ran to 08:07Z, not 05:16Z.**
   cc-258 closed its session inside it. The startup-timeout buckets cleared
   twice (04:45, 05:45Z), and that is what made it look like two.
2. **The trigger is the ec-crawl's ecs-windows stage, not platform-counts.**
   Platform-counts is a contributor and a victim (H2).
3. **The box has two disks, and root (swap) reads as much as /data.** The
   prompt expected one data device. Item 1 rates /data and sums every whole
   disk for the budget.
4. **The metrics endpoint caches for 60 s.** A rate needs the exporter's clock
   (Σ cpu seconds), not the route's. The prompt's "Δ`io_time_seconds` / 60"
   would have read 0 % and 184 % on alternate samples.
5. **The 88 % never became 100 %**, yet the box throttled. The dashboard is not
   the instance bucket's live level.
6. **FIX-1353 (dispatcher s23) as filed conflicts with this census.** It
   narrows gate (d) for class P to the live box plus 10 min. The 11-02 clean
   buckets would have opened it mid-throttle. Build it with or after the
   `io:` rung (noted in s25 and the audit §8; the bullets are append-only, so
   neither was edited).
7. Platform-counts' registry `cap_mb` is **64** (FIX-1357), not 128.
8. **FIX-1345's bot ran 10 h 06 min late** (created 20:06:28Z for `0 10 * * 1`).
   GitHub's schedule queue did that, not the workflow. 10-26's ran 6 h 40 min
   late.
9. `check:job-registry` printed **6 WARNs**: the new FIX-1373 bands vs the
