## 4. What contradicted the bullets or this prompt
- **The status routes' Data Cache isn't "their design".** Commit `22c9563c` meant a 5-minute edge cache, but `getIp(request)` on the tracking proxy makes `revalidate = 300` inert. The reads were never cached and the routes carry no edge-cache header of their own. Whether they *should* be cached is a separate question and isn't filed.
- **Is `cache()` request-scoped in a Route Handler? No.** It's a call-through outside an RSC render (`resolveCache()` → `new Map()`), read from the vendored react-server source. The sink is page-only, as the prompt allowed for.
- **Does a throw give a 5xx on any of the 12 pages? Yes.** It does on both measured pages, with and without `loading.tsx`, and so does `noStore()`. The premise from `reference_nextjs_redirect_under_suspense` (a throw under Suspense streams a 200) holds for dynamic renders, **not** for an ISR render, which Next buffers whole before caching. So D2's either/or collapsed: `noStore()` alone gives exactly "throw so Next keeps the last good page".
- **The D2 premise that `noStore()` serves the degraded page uncached is wrong on ISR routes.** It ends the render. The STOP condition ("noStore() does NOT change Cache-Control") didn't fire, because it did change it, to `private, no-store` on a 500. The consequences:

  - After FIX-1227, a whale or degraded first render on any of the 8 ISR pages is a **bare Next 500 page**, not an empty-looking page. Every revalidation keeps the good page. Recorded in FIX-1229.
- **Which of the 14 GET+POST routes had the defect? None**, by module-level `revalidate = 0`, quoted in read 2. Two cookie routes are also GET+PATCH.
- **The 36 was never the whole defect class.** It includes `/api/browse` through `execute.ts`, and excludes the GET+POST routes, which never had it.
- **front-door-watch was protected by request order:** its Logs fetch is `cache: "no-store"` and runs before its insert. It's now explicit.
