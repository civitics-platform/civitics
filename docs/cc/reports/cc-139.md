## 6. What contradicted the design

### (a) The expected close the measurement refused — FIX-969

The prompt expected `closes-as-superseded` with an explicit gate: *"IF read 3
shows 26 clean for 30 days; otherwise APPEND with 26's numbers and keep open."*
**26 is not clean** — 2 of 4 firings failed (08-25 `job startup timeout`, 09-01
watchdog cancel at 5,409.8 s against 5,400). The honest reading is finely
balanced: under the bullet's *own* shape — blowing the 6 h ceiling — 26 is
spotless, and so is every other job on the box. But the gate was written as
"clean", 26 is not, and a second reason surfaced that the prompt did not
anticipate: **jobid 17 has zero successes in its last four firings**, both recent
legs dead on `server restarted`. Closing a bullet that names a job which never
succeeds would have been premature. Appended and left open, with the receipts for
a cheap close next pass.

### (b) `cron.job_run_details` retention is 83 days, not ~30

Rule 140 says ~30 days. The table retains **2026-06-29 → present**, 56,931 rows.
This made FIX-969's "full retained window" comparison possible against the
original 2026-06-29→08-06 measurement rather than approximate. It also means
"nothing since X" claims sourced from this table can reach further back than the
rule assumes — worth correcting in the template, since a 30-day assumption would
have made me *narrow* several reads unnecessarily.

### (c) The FIX-1188 census question was mis-shaped at the source

Not a tree-versus-prompt conflict — the prompt faithfully relayed the bullet —
but the bullet's three-way split (FE row / resolves / unminted) cannot apply to a
