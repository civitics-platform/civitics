## Item 3 — FIX-1348: the backfill shape, and the first rows

Each tick reads the newest `front_door_watch` row's `started_at` before its
INSERT. The read runs after the alert, uses `idx_data_sync_log_pipeline
(pipeline, started_at DESC)`, aborts at 5 s and is best-effort. The tick then
writes `metadata.backfill`:

- `null` when no tick is missing. The key is always present.
- `{error}` when the read failed. The INSERT is still attempted.
- `{missed_ticks, from, to, buckets, state, reason, mode, state_store}` when
  ticks are missing:
  - `missed_ticks` is the gap in whole 15-minute periods, minus one.
  - `from`/`to` are the newest earlier row and this tick.
  - `buckets` are the four this tick already holds. There is no Upstash read.

The logic is pure in `apps/civitics/src/lib/front-door-breadcrumb.ts`.
`front-door-watch-backfill.test.ts` has 6 tests. It replays 10-25 with the
13:45 row's real buckets: 11:15:28.563 → 13:45:28.228 is 9 missed ticks,
carrying 12:45, 13:00 and 13:15 RED. It also covers the prompt's three cases.
The prompt's "2 h gap → 8" is read as two hours with no row (8 ticks dark,
rows 2h15m apart). Rows exactly 2 h apart are 7 missed ticks.

**Local:** a server with Upstash and email pointed at dead endpoints. Tick 1
had no earlier row, so its `backfill` was `null`. The row was then moved back
2h15m, and tick 2 gave `missed_ticks 8`, `from 2026-10-31T22:07:20.643Z`,
`to 2026-11-01T00:22:27.543Z`, 4 buckets, `mode off`, `state_store
unreachable: upstash: fetch failed`. The two synthetic local rows were then
deleted.
