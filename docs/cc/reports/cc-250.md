# cc-250 — Sunday's front-door outage was a swap-ceiling wedge; no job of ours was the owner

Audit: `docs/audits/2026-10-25-io-budget-front-door-triage.md`. All reads
were taken 17:30–18:00Z on prod, read-only. **C** marks an instant Craig
reported by email; everything else was read.

## §1 Verdict

1. **Shape (B): a memory wedge, with the IO budget as the mechanism and
   Supabase's daily backup as the final reader.** `dispatch_log.box` reads
   swap at **920–1024 MB of 1024** from 21:00Z Saturday to its last sample at
   11:15Z Sunday, with 1024 MB / MemAvailable 214 MB / load1 18.39 at 09:05Z.
   pg_cron starved in steps: 155 `job startup timeout` rows (the only failure
   class) at 03:02, 04:33–05:05, 09:02 and 10:02–10:10, then continuously from
   11:25:00Z. Postgres logged `checkpoint starting: immediate force wait` at
   **11:21:55Z**. It logs one at 11:20–11:56Z on 7 of 7 retained days, which is
   the daily backup start. The data front door read 52x from the 11:30 bucket
   through 13:15. The runner-up is (A). It would need our load to have been
   heavier than the clean 10-18/10-19 (it was lighter: 3,754 s of non-monitor
   cron wall against 4,070 and 5,037 s), or the swap to have had headroom (it
   had none).
2. **Restart class: an unclean, Supabase-side VM reboot at 13:25:52Z.**
   `pg_postmaster_start_time()` = 13:25:53.305Z. The log shows `not properly
   shut down; automatic recovery in progress` and no fast shutdown, and the
   kernel counters restarted. Nothing of ours issued it: the front-door arm is
   configured `arm`, but its Upstash store was capped, so it failed safe, and
   Craig did no hand restart (**C**).
3. **Owners of the spend.** None of ours was in flight at 11:21:55Z or at 12:00Z
   (the last unit ended 11:20:10Z). The largest spenders of ours before that
   are in §3 below. The one foreign reader was Supabase's backup. The variable
   that broke 10-25 and not 10-18 is **swap creep**: daily average swap was 913
   MB on 10-18 and 989 MB on 10-24, and samples at ≥ 1000 MB went from 6 a day
   to 63, over 17 days of uptime.
4. **Recovery: the reboot.** It freed memory (swap 183 MB at 13:34Z) and ended
   the backup. The disk stayed slow for about 10 min: exporter directory
   listings took 32–37 s at 13:27–13:28Z, and the dispatcher's last failed tick
   was 13:35:18Z. The probe read clean from the 13:45 bucket, and the watchdog
   logged `recovered` at 14:00:28Z.

## §3 The spend ledger in summary

three biggest spenders of ours, all in the 6.5 h before the backup:

1. **ec-crawl `rebuild_entity_connections_external`**, the weekly ungate:
   07:21:00–07:40:39Z, 1,179 s, 577,603 rows. It ran the same size on 10-18
   with no stall.

drain cannot be read (Craig's screenshots carry it).

## §5 What contradicted the prompt

1. **R1:** the expected 10-08 epoch did not hold. The postmaster started
   **13:25:53Z today**, after an **unclean** shutdown (crash recovery), so (B)
   was in play from the first read.
2. **R2:** `restart.mode` reads **`arm`** on every row, where the prompt said
   it was disarmed until 11-01. It could not act, because `state_store` was
   `unreachable: upstash 400 … Limit 500000, Usage 500001`. No `down` row exists
   in the DB: nine ticks are missing, 11:30–13:30.
3. **The ring:** it was blind as expected, but **`dispatch_log.box` is a ring
   substitute** the prompt didn't name. It holds the on-box memory mirror at
   1–6 samples per 15 min, and it carries the verdict.
4. **R7(a):** the status half lands at **:01/:31**, not :07/:37.
5. **R7(b):** the Vercel metric that crossed 100 % is `included_usage_usd`,
   which is the calendar-month *projection* (= `projected_usage_usd`
   20.0028). It stepped once at 07:01 when Vercel's daily charge ingest landed.
   It was not a night burst.
6. **R11:** every cumulative view dates from 13:25:53Z, not from 10-08.
   `track_io_timing` is off, so the per-call read time the prompt asked for is
   0 everywhere.
7. **R6:** `pipeline_state` has no `prod_session` or `monthly_egress_estimate`
   key on prod.
8. **R4:** `unit_rows` is null on every row. `unit_ms` is on reaped rows only.
9. **R5:** the cron and dispatched VACUUMs write no `data_sync_log` row, so
   their walls come from the cron ledger.
10. **R8:** `supavisor_logs` had no row at all, and `postgrest_logs` none from
    11:30 to 13:30, so log shipping was dark inside the wedge. No `pg_dump`,
    `wal-g` or walsender connection is ever logged; the backup shows only as
    the checkpointer's `immediate force wait`.
11. **Pacing slip:** one Logs API call landed at about 17:45:05Z, inside the
    :45 +0–1 window. The throwaway script checked the clock and then slept. I
    fixed it to sleep first; that minute made 4 calls, under the 10/min limit.
12. The `session:held` line has no `label_stale` field.
13. The box reads MemTotal 904 MiB. The prompt's "Small tier 1,000 IOPS / 22
