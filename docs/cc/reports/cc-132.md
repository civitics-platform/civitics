## 9. What contradicted the design

### (a) Item 1's stop condition is unsatisfiable — I proceeded, and this is the call to review

The prompt says: *"STOP item 1 if read 5 finds no slot ≥ 60 min clear on both
sides in 02:00–04:15."* Read literally, **that fires**, and I did not stop.

Three daily jobs sit inside or on the edge of that range — `vote-stats-refresh`
03:30, `platform-counts-daily` 03:53, `abuse-events-retention` 04:15 — and they
are 23 and 22 minutes apart. For 60 minutes clear *after*, a slot must be
≤ 02:30; for 60 minutes clear *before* `dpr-vacuum-analyze` at 02:00 on Sun/Wed,
it must be ≥ 03:00. No daily slot satisfies both on every day of the week.

I proceeded for two reasons, and they should be checked rather than taken:

1. **The prompt's own Design of Record cites rule 137** — a gate is checked for
   satisfiability against the schedule it sits in. A gate that no slot can
   satisfy is a bug in the gate, which is exactly the class cc-131 fixed for the
   `partial` and "≤ 5,000 dead" gates.
2. **The platform does not use that standard for vacuum placement.** FIX-1152
   put `abuse-events-retention`, `ec-vacuum-analyze` and `fe-vacuum-analyze` at
   04:15, 04:30 and 04:50 — **15 and 20 minutes apart**. The 90-minute rule in
   gate (a) is about how long after a vacuum a *landing* may start; it is not a
   spacing rule between vacuums, and the STOP appears to have inherited it by
   analogy.

What I actually shipped is the prompt's own prescribed default, `0 3 * * *`,
whose condition for the alternative never fired. Its real clearance is 26 min to
`vote-stats-refresh` daily and 6 min to `group-donor-rollup-refresh` on
Wednesdays, against a 227.2 s worst measured wall. If that is too thin, the
lever is a slot nearer 02:30 on a day-of-week-aware schedule — say it and I will
move it; it is one `cron.alter_job`.

### (d) A sub-second `ec-crawl` unit does not mean the arm is caught up

The drain-and-wait convention says "EC units back to baseline". That signal is

asymmetry: only `backoff` writes a row; `blackout` and `cycle_cooldown` are

at 19:33:21 was `cycle_cooldown`, *"last cycle closed 256 min ago; minimum
interval is 360 min"*.

satisfiable form is the instrument's own vocabulary — `ec_crawl_gate()->>'reason'`
plus the `entity_connections_donations` watermark — which is rule 137 applied to
