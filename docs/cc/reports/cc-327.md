## Decisions that changed, and why

- **D6, one deviation, with evidence: the vote path's placeholder
  `introduced_at` is replaced.** A measure minted by a roll call carries the
  bill number as its title and the roll's date as its introduced date

  1,390 number-titled federal measures have `introduced_at` equal to their
  first vote's date. Every measure the first live run filled a title on had a
  source date that differed (e.g. 119-S-2403: stored 2026-09-16, its first
  vote; source 2025-07-23).
  - Strict D6 would have left a wrong date on about prod's 1,258 number-titled
    measures. The prompt's "3,354 with no introduced date" undercounts the
    measures whose date is wrong.
  - The rule: while the title is still exactly the bill number, a stored date
    equal to the measure's first vote's date is replaced. Every other stored
    date is kept and counted. Outcome `replaced_placeholder`; run 2 shows it
    on all 10.
  - **This is the one place the walker overwrites a stored value. Craig
    should ratify it or take it out before a prod fill.**
- **D4:** the key carries the committee codes (above). `source_key` is
  NOT NULL.
