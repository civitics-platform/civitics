**Where the callers changed beyond the lookups.** Read 0 asked what each caller does with a nominee or court it could not resolve. Under D2, two of those answers became wrong writes:

- **Nominee.** An unresolved nominee `continue`s, so it is missing from `currentOfficialIds`. `closeStaleConnections` then closes that nominee's open appointment edge. That is an update caused by a failed read. Now an agency with any failed nominee skips its stale-close and logs one line.
- **Court.** A judge whose court is missing from the map was given `[...courtGovBodyMap.values()][0]!`, the map's first court (SCOTUS). That rewrites the judge's `governing_body_id`. Now a judge whose court failed tonight is skipped and counted (`judges_court_unresolved`). The fallback for court slugs outside the 14 is unchanged.
