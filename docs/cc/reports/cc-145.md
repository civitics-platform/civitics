## 6. What contradicted cc-141 §3, cc-143 §5, the bullets, or this prompt

1. **The prompt's crash time.** "09-22 ~14:30 UTC … precedes [jobid 17] …
   decisive: it cannot be the common factor." The log is normal to 15:08:40,
   and jobid 17 started at 15:00:00. The exclusion rested on a time the log
   refutes. That flipped the verdict.
2. **"Down ~8 h" was not a crash.** Postgres was alive throughout: pg_cron
   wrote rows, and jobid 17 committed its own `partial` row at 18:07:58.
   There is no `interrupted`, and the only terminating event is the fast
   shutdown. What was down was the forker, the ingress (522) and the log
   shipping.
3. **cc-141 §3 read 11(e).** "Seven restarts across five weekdays … no
   causation is required." Sound on its inputs. A third consecutive Tuesday,
   and a degradation that lifts ten minutes after the statement's cancel,
   overturn it.
4. **The FIX-1112 header.** "Cost is independent of the lag: ~350-420 s on
   prod." The two full-mode runs that got going since took 21,628.6 s and
   11,278 s. The 350–420 s were July's successes, before FR grew.
5. **The prompt's retention arithmetic.** "gone Tue 09-29 ~15:09 UTC" for
   09-15's log. Retention is a rolling 7 × 24 h from *now*, so it was already
   gone when the prompt was written. Read 2(c) could not run.
6. **§B D1's premise.** "the restart re-zeroed `pg_stat_user_indexes`." It did
   not: a clean fast shutdown persists cumulative stats. The script's gate was
   wrong about its own epoch, not the evidence. The reason string in the
   prompt's runbook would have recorded a false statement, so it was corrected
   with Craig's agreement.
7. **`pg_stat_statements_info.stats_reset` and the pgstat epoch diverged.**
   pgss reset at 22:32:13 while the index counters survived. So pgss
   `stats_reset` is not a safe proxy for the index-counter epoch after a sick
   shutdown. The project memory saying it was "the reliable one" is corrected.
8. **FIX-1208's framing and FIX-1207's no-op closure.** 1208 is titled "…P2-A
   works but cannot be verified by that instrument". A third instrument
   (`edge_logs`) says P2-A very likely does not work at all. FIX-1207 closed
   as no-op on the belief that the route was firing into this DB. That belief
   now looks wrong. 1207 stays closed; the finding lives on 1208. Craig may
   want 1208 re-rated above 🟡, because the watchdog's second path has been
   logging all-clear from a payload that is not this database's.
9. **The prompt's gate list omitted (f).** It was read anyway, and it failed
   twice on post-restart cold-cache residue before passing at 00:02. Worth
   keeping in the header of any prompt that lands a prod op within hours of a
   restart.
