  - `/donors/*` and `/proposals/*` go to `entity_pages`; `/jurisdictions/*` and
    `/officials/*` go to `entity_leaf`.
  - On 11-10 those were 931 leaf + 1,528 pages = 2,459 checks, **~88 % of §11's B (2,805)**.

  - At ~1 page per IP, each check under the new per-client key is first-in-window: 3
    commands, not 2.13. The owed k receipt should expect ~3.
- **`--origin` under-counts beacon work.** Fleet web-vitals at the edge on 11-10 were

  real origin arrivals on 11-10 were ~29k, not 23k.

  origin header, so a literal "only on `{pass, match}`" could never show `key=cf` on the
  health path. Read 5 expects it there.

  - `origin-auth.test.ts` asserts it equals `{pass, match}` under `enforce` for every
    input combination (540) on non-cron paths.
  - The two differ only on `/api/cron/*`, where nothing is bucketed.
  - It is still never true under `report` / `off` / not-production / no-secret, and never
    on the alias (no header).
- **D6: all three dimensions were refused.** `clientAsn`, `clientASNDescription` and
  `clientRefererHost` each returned "does not have access to the field". Only
  `--ua-like` shipped, and the header names the refusals.
