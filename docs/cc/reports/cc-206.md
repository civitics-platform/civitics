## §1 The decision table as built, and the replays

`decideRestart` (`packages/db/src/front-door-restart.ts`) applies these rules in
order. The first rule that matches decides:

| # | condition | action | reason (abridged) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | verdict ≠ `down` | none | `front door not DOWN (<state>)`, and the caller DELs `down_since` + `would_restart_sent` |
| 0b | the Upstash read failed | none | `state store unreachable (…) — fail safe, no restart` |
| 1 | `downSince == null` | none | `first DOWN tick — the hold starts now`, and the caller `SET … NX EX 86400` |
| 2 | held < 30 min | none | `held N min, threshold 30` |
| 3 | probe answered | skip | `the direct probe answers — the Logs half alone never restarts` |
| 4 | last restart < 60 min ago | skip | `last restart N min ago — spacing 60 min` |
| 5 | ≥ 2 restarts in 24 h | skip | `cap 2/24 h — a loop is worse than an outage` |
| 6 | mode `off` | none | |
| 7 | mode `report`, or `arm` without the token | would_restart | the second form names `SUPABASE_MANAGEMENT_RESTART_KEY` |
| 8 | otherwise | restart | `DOWN N min, probe dark — restarting; health …` |

The health read appears in every reason from rule 3 on (`health
healthy|unhealthy|unknown`), and no rule reads it. A test pins that: the same
inputs give `restart` under all three health values.

`FRONT_DOOR_AUTO_RESTART` is read as follows:
- unset or empty → `report`;
- `off`, `report` or `arm` (case- and space-tolerant) → that mode;
- anything else → `report`, with a `mode_warning`. A typo cannot arm it.

**Every POST attempt counts toward the spacing and the cap,** whatever it
answered. A refused token is therefore tried at most twice a day, not every
15 minutes.

## §4 What contradicted the design

1. **The probe premise. This is the headline, and it decides whether arming
   does anything.** D1's rule 3 (probe answered → skip) assumes the direct
   probe goes dark during a wedge. Two things in the tree say it did not on
   10-06:
   - **The edge counts.** The triage audit's `edge_logs` table (§6) shows the
     non-5xx count per 30 min through the wedge as 1, 2, 1, 2, 2, 2, 1, 0, 2,
     2, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, from 16:30 to 00:00. That is about **2 per half hour
     = the watchdog's two ticks**. Everything else got 52x.
   - **The page cadence.** The prompt says the DOWN pages came **"on the
     hour"**. The probe arm pages every tick (`isDownEdge: true`, read 2); only
     the Logs arm pages hourly.

   One reading fits both: the probe's keyless `GET /rest/v1/` was answered
   (<500, likely the gateway's 401) by a layer in front of the wedge.

   **I built D1 as ratified.** Report mode means nothing fires either way. The
   replays pin both models: with the probe answering, **no series restarts**.
   I did not run the one `edge_logs` read that would settle it, because it was
   outside the posture. Two cheaper settlers:
   - The subject of any 10-06 DOWN email in Craig's inbox: `direct probe got
     no answer in 3 attempt(s)` means probe-dark; `the two newest closed
     buckets are both >= 50% …` means the probe answered.
   - The next wedge's DOWN emails, which now carry `Auto-restart … skip — the
     direct probe answers` or produce a WOULD FIRE.

   If the probe answered, the rule-3 decision belongs to Cowork. The 52x
   calibration gap (healthy ≤ 7.1 %, wedged ≥ 95.8 %) is the evidence for
   letting the Logs arm alone carry a restart after the hold.
2. **"08-31 → exactly one restart across the 17 h"** does not follow from D1.
   The fixture is history: no restart happened, so the wedge runs on, and the
   ratified 60-min spacing plus the cap of 2 allow **two** (06:47 and 07:47).
   10-06 is the same (17:02 and 18:02). "Exactly one" would need a cap of 1. I
   kept the ratified cap and asserted two.
3. **"Six healthy days → zero"** does not hold: the HEALTHY fixture contains
   the real 08-29 FIX-1125 outage (4 RED buckets). Under the probe-dark model,
   D1 restarts it once at 06:47, 28 minutes before it cleared by itself.
   - The five other days and the 09-01 statement-timeout window give zero
     under both models. Asserted so.
   - The 30-min hold does not filter a ~60-min self-clearing outage. A 60-min
     hold would have; that trades against a slower restart for every real
     wedge.
4. **Does `state: "down"` already imply ≥ 30 min of red?** For the **Logs arm,
   yes**: two RED *closed* buckets mean at least 30 min of RED before the DOWN
   tick, so the Logs-arm DOWN is 32 min after the wedge went total on both
   series (06:15 → 06:47, 16:30 → 17:02). With the hold on top, a Logs-arm
   restart would come ~62 min after onset.

   **But rule 3 means the Logs arm alone never restarts.** A restart needs the
   probe arm, which fires on the first tick inside the wedge. So the hold IS
   the latency: **measured onset → restart = 32 min** on 08-31 (total 06:15) and
   on 10-06 (first RED quarter 16:30). Against the box's death at 16:04Z it is
   58 min, because the edge only went fully RED at 16:30 under the even split.

   **Jitter.** `down_since` stores the raw tick instant. A real Vercel tick
   that runs a few seconds earlier than the one before can read 29.9 min held
   and defer to +45. The replays use exact :02 ticks.
5. **The Upstash wrapper.** There was no generic command helper. Rather than
   add `upstash-kv.ts` with a second copy of `upstash()`'s error handling, I
   renamed and exported the existing private helper as `upstashRequest`, which
   touches three lines of `box-health-store.ts`. State reads are one `MGET`
   (four keys, one command, ~2,900 a month), not separate `GET`s. A healthy
   tick writes nothing.
6. **`maxDuration`** went 30 → 60, for the reasons in read 6. A
   pre-existing property surfaced here: `sendEmail`'s counter write is
   unbounded and goes through the front door. That is why the POST and its
   state write go **before** any email.
7. **WOULD FIRE "at most once per outage", with key EX 6 h.** A 17-h outage
   sends three (06:47, 12:47, 18:47); the key is also deleted on the first
   non-DOWN tick. Report mode does not simulate the spacing or the cap: it
   says `would_restart` on every held tick (the email is deduplicated), and
   the armed sequence is in the replays.
8. **The prompt's own access rules disagree.** "Prod access" says "No
   `db-query --prod`", but item 2's verification is a `db-query --prod` read. I
   followed item 2 with one read-only query, polled every 45 s from ~02:57Z.
9. **`forker.md` "step s11".** The plan has s1–s7 only; no s8–s10 exist in the
   tree. s11 was added as named, with a paragraph.
10. **FIX-1130's bullet lives in `docs/archive/fixes-archive.md`**, not in
