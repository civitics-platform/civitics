- **The prompt credited the `_conn` fix to FIX-1112.** FIX-1112 is the watermark-ratchet census; the fix was **FIX-1123**: materialize + `ANALYZE` + 16 windows. The 200 estimate survives inside each window.
- **Whether arm timings are stamped, and where.** Yes, in two places:
