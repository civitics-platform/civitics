## §3 What contradicted the design

1. **08-29 is not excluded by the 30-min hold.** This is the stop, detailed in
   §1. D2's sentence "a 15-min hold would restart it" is also off: a 15-min
   hold restarts it at 07:02, and the 30-min hold at 07:17.
2. **The hold sits on the tick lattice.** The prod ticks fire at
   :28.408–:28.603. `down_since` stores the tick's own `Date.now()`, so at the
   third DOWN tick `now − downSince` is 30 min ± a few hundred ms. Either:
   - `held < 30` passes, and the restart is at the 3rd tick (62 min; 08-29
     restarts); or
   - it fails, and the restart is at the 4th tick (77 min; 08-29 excluded,
     because 07:32 is not DOWN).

   That is a coin flip, on every wedge and on every 08-29-shaped event. cc-206
   §4.4 flagged the jitter for latency; the 08-29 consequence is new here.
3. **"Assert they now agree" across both probe models** does not hold. The
   probe-dark model declares DOWN 30 min earlier through the probe arm (06:17
   on 08-31), so it restarts 30 min earlier. That doesn't change the
   conclusion: the answers model is the measured one.
4. **The `isDownEdge` plumbing and the DOWN-email line needed nothing.** D1 is
   a `decideRestart` change plus reason text. The route already passes
   `probe.answered`, and `restartLine()` prints whatever reason it gets.
5. **The arming timeline.** The prompt says prod was armed at ~20:30 PDT
   (03:30Z). The stamps read `report` through 03:45Z, and `arm` from 04:00Z.
