## Decisions that changed, and why

- **V2, the result's order.** The prompt said `vote_rolls.result` first. The
  page prints a detailed row's own result first, then the stored per-roll
  `vote_result`, then a session list's short form.
  - Read 3: HR 1's 44 Senate rolls are list-only, and the list says "Rejected"
    where the stored result, read by the nightly from the same per-roll file,
    says "Amendment Rejected".
  - Read 15: of 297 list-only Senate rolls with a stored result, 288 are the
    long form of the list's words and 9 a synonym ("Bill Defeated" for
    "Rejected", "Motion to Table Failed" for "Rejected"). None names another
    outcome.
  - With the prompt's order, a roll would read "Rejected" today and "Amendment
    Rejected" once the walker read its file.
- **V6** compares titles ignoring periods as well as case and whitespace (the
  `metadataTitle` rule), so "H.R. 1" equals "HR 1".
- **V4.** A requirement with no result reads as its own capitalised line
  ("Three-fifths required").
- **V9** uses typographic quotes around “Recorded here”.
- **B1, which measures make the reads.**
  - The three reads run only for a Congress number plus a bill type the walker
    reads (HR, S, HRES, SRES, HJRES, SJRES, HCONRES, SCONRES). A nomination
    carries a congress_gov ref too, and the walker skips it, so it would
    otherwise print B7's sentence for good.
  - The ref read keeps only `^\d+-TYPE-\d+$` refs and prefers a stamped one.
    It is `React.cache`d in `get-proposal.ts` and shared with
    `generateMetadata`, which needs the short title for `<title>`.
  - The cosponsor read has no source filter.
- **B1, the sponsor.** The sponsor comes by embed on `bill_details` (one
  `officials_pkey` probe), and is linked only when that official is the one
  `detail.sponsor_official_id` names. Otherwise the line prints the source's
  name, unlinked. A bill_details sponsor that an earlier writer set, and that
  differs from the source's, is never linked as the source's.
- **B3.** The source's name carries its own tag ("Rep. Arrington, Jodey C.
  [R-TX-19]"), which is stripped when the tag is printed. District 0 prints
  "At Large", as Congress.gov does.
- **B4's wording:**
  - "297 of 302 on record here, by party: D 150 · R 147" when fewer;
    "On record here, by party: …" when equal or over.
  - The list's summary is "List the N on record", plus "and K withdrawn" when
    some withdrew.
  - Party abbreviations follow the members list's D / R / I.
- **B5.**
  - The "× N" rule comes from read 5(b).
  - When the printed lines are fewer than the source's `action_count`, one
    sentence says why: "Congress.gov lists some actions more than once, under
    each chamber's system or each committee; each is printed once here."
- **B2 also reaches** the breadcrumb's last crumb and the share button's title,
  the two other places the page names itself. JSON-LD `name` keeps
  `proposals.title`.
- **A timeout is still degraded.** A new read that times out is recorded as
  degraded (FIX-1227), like every read on this page. That render is not cached:
  a 500 on a first render, the last good page on a revalidation. The wording
  module's "no row → today's lines" covers a miss and a non-timeout error.

## What the officials page needs for "bills sponsored"

The reads are cheap and need no migration. `bill_details.primary_sponsor_id`
is indexed (`…_primary_sponsor`), and so is `proposal_cosponsors.official_id`
(`proposal_cosponsors_official_id`).

Three things are missing:

1. **The fill, whole.** 1,249 of 9,782 refs are stamped, at about 150 a run
   every two hours or so, which is about 12-03 or 12-04. Until then a list
   drawn from part of the measures would read as the member's count.
2. **A denominator.** The per-measure stamp has no per-member count.
   Congress.gov's `/member/{bioguide}/sponsored-legislation` and
   `/cosponsored-legislation` carry one, and that is a new read for a walker,
   not the page.
3. **The unresolved cosponsors.** The 504 measures short of their source count
   mean a member who did not resolve has no rows at all. That is mostly the
   117th and 118th Congresses. The page should say "N of M, per Congress.gov",
   as the bill page does.

The walker sets `primary_sponsor_id` only when it is NULL. So a member's
sponsored list should be read where `primary_sponsor_id` agrees with
`detail.sponsor_official_id`, or the page will count a sponsor that an earlier
writer set.
