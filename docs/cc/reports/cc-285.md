## Decisions I changed, and why

- **D4's cap lives in the table, not the route.** D2's insert-own policy is a
  second write path: the publishable key plus a user's JWT, straight to
  PostgREST. It never passes the route's checks. A route-only cap is cosmetic
  against anyone who reads the bundle. The trigger counts on D1's index ("counted
  on the table"), and the route still answers the 429. The lib keeps the literals.
  `data-flags.test.ts` pins them to the migration: the cap, the window, `53400`,
  and the three CHECK lists.
- **D2, tightened.** The `authenticated` INSERT grant is column-scoped to
  `entity_type, entity_id, source, reason, comment, flagger_user_id`. A direct
  insert therefore cannot back-date `created_at` out of the cap's window,
  pre-stamp a resolution, or set a classification that keeps the row out of the
  queue. SQL CASE 3 covers all three. The policies are `TO authenticated`, as D2
  says; `content_flags`' are `TO public` (read 2).
- **D1.** I dropped the separate `(entity_type, entity_id)` index, because the
  unique key's two leading columns serve the same lookups. I added
  `classification_confidence` 0..1 and `source` 1..64 CHECKs: the 0.8 queue
  threshold implies the first, and the second bounds a free-text key.
- **D4's map.** It existed in two private copies, not one: the attribution route
  and `packages/db/src/attribution.ts`. Both now read `ATTRIBUTION_ENTITY_TABLE`
  (+ `isAttributionEntityType`) from `@civitics/db`, and so does the new route.
- **D4's event.** `flag_create` carries `targetId` = the new flag's id, with the
  entity, its type and the reason in `meta`. A cap hit records `cap_hit`
  (`route data_flags, cap data_flag_daily`), as the comments route does. D4 did
  not say what a cap hit records.
- **D6.** `resolveEndpointNames` takes an optional specs map. Its default is
  unchanged, so the promotion and disputes queues read exactly as before. Only
  the data-flags queue adds proposals (`title`). `resolve_data_flag` answers
  **409** once a flag is resolved: the update filters on
  `admin_resolved_at IS NULL`.
- **D5.** The explorer rail uses an `inline` variant, because a scrolling rail
  clips an absolute popover. The pages pass the loaded row's id (`official.id`,
  `p.id`) rather than `params.id` (FIX-1306's rule).
